Watch: Delia Smith says data centre will be bad for county

Watch: Delia Smith says data centre will be bad for county

Television cook and author Delia Smith has warned a new data centre in Norfolk would do nothing for the local community.

Campaigners fear the £1.2bn proposed centre at Keswick, on the edge of Norwich, will create noise pollution and have a negative impact on nearby Marston Marsh.

Smith, honorary life president and former majority shareholder of Norwich City FC, said "it's going to be bad", while TV presenter Jake Humphrey added the countryside was "not for sale".

Developer Norwich Apex said an ecological impact assessment concluded no effect on the reserve was expected.
