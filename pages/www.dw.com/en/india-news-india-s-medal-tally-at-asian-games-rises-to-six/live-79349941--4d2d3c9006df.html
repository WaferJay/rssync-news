India studying US tariff on Russian oil buyers, trade minister says

The Indian government is studying the details of US tariffs on buyers of Russian oil, Indian Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said on Monday. 

Goyal's comments came days after Washington passed and sanctions and tariffs bill allowing US President Donald Trump to impose stiff tariffs of up to 100% on India, China and other countries which continue to depend on Russian energy.

India-US ties facing test

While India and Russia ties have grown stronger since the start of the war in Ukraine, driven largely by Russian oil exports, the same cannot be said of India-US relations under Prime Minister Narendra Modi as tensions mounted over New Delhi's purchases of Russian oil.

Last August, Trump imposed an additional 25% tariff on Indian imports over those purchases, on top of a 25% reciprocal tariff.

However, the additional Russia-related tariff was removed in February 2026, while the reciprocal tariff was reduced to 18% as part of a US-India trade framework.

That landmark bilateral trade agreement between the two countries remains in limbo, with no significant breakthrough and experts argue that Washington is likely to use the threat of additional tariffs as leverage for a favorable deal with India.

India's premier tech institute apologizes after alleged student suicide

The Indian Institute of Technology, one of the country's premier Institutes, issued an apology on Monday for its earlier communication regarding an alleged student suicide which has led to protests.

"The details and circumstances leading up to the incident remain subject to investigation by the appropriate authorities. In view of the ongoing investigation, it was inappropriate to have set out or characterized details..." the institute said in a post on X. 

A student suicide and what followed

A second-year student of the Department of Energy Science and Engineering reportedly died on September 18. A day later, the institute issued a statement on social media saying the 20-year-old died by suicide after he was found using a mobile phone in the examination hall, allegedly attempting to seek answers from ChatGPT. 

He was found dead in his room later that evening, it added. 

Students at IIT Bombay have since staged protests demanding an investigation and action against the professor suprevising the exam. The deceased student's family had also reportedly named the same professor in the police investigation, alleging harassment and caste discrimination.

On September 20, IIT Bombay's Director, Professor Shireesh Kedare, met protesting students and later suspended the professor in question while he was being investigated, Indian media reported.

Viral video shows group of men grabbing minor girl, police responds

Videos showing a group of men in the eastern state of Bihar harassing two minors, including a girl, were going viral on social media on Monday.

The initial video, seemingly shot at night, showed a group confronting a young man and woman, apparently because they were together alone. One of the perpetrators is seen grabbing the girl from the bottom, hoisting her in the air, to rush her away from her companion. 

More videos of the incident surfaced later, showing the couple surrounded by a mob while they were visibly distressed. We are not including the video because of its sensitive nature.

Indians demand arrests

Enraged netizens reacted to the video, tagging local police and demanding accountability and arrests.

"The video from Jamui, Bihar is deeply disturbing," Indian opposition leader Rahul Gandhi wrote on social media. "Two Class 10 students were stopped on a road. Harassed. Humiliated. Molested. A young girl, hands folded, begged to be let go while goons groped her and dragged her away," he said. 

"Every individual in that video must be identified and prosecuted under POCSO, and justice must be swift. And if the incident happened on September 19th, why are the culprits not behind bars yet?" he added. POCSO stands for the Protection of Children from Sexual Offenses Act.

How have police responded?

Local police confirmed that the two victims were minors who had gone to the location to take pictures, and that a criminal complaint (First Information Report or FIR) had been made. "The accused will be identified and arrests ensured at the earliest," Jamui Police said on social media. 

Meanwhile, Jamui SP Vishwajeet Dayal said the video came to the department's attention on Monday morning.

"Based on the video, we identified the victims and recorded their statements. It came to light that the incident took place around 7 pm on September 19. The two friends, a boy and a girl, had gone for coaching and had gone to the area, where the incident occurred, to take photographs," the officer told reporters. 

"The accused have been identified. We are taking legal action and the accused will be arrested soon," Dayal said.

Delhi's VIPs under scanner amid electoral roll revision

Delhi's revision of electoral rolls is in the spotlight once again, with several VIPs in government and politics receiving notices for "discrepancies" in their submitted voter Information. 

That includes India's Foreign Minister Subrahmanyam Jaishankar, India's Election Commissioner Sukhbir Singh Sandhu, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri and several former leaders, both from the Ruling Bharatiya Janata Party and the opposition, Indian media reported on Monday. 

Delhi's Chief Minister Rekha Gupta also received a notice for an age discrepancy, the Times of India reported. 

SIR, or the Special Intensive Revision, is an exercise conducted by the Election Commission of India (ECI) to remove redundancies in India's electoral rolls. The discrepancies arising from this process often include mismatches in names, family details or age, which can be rectified by providing a list of pre-approved documents to local officers. 

A controversial process

The SIR has been a controversial exercise over the last few years. The latest round of SIR was announced by the ECI in October 2025 and several states have undergone the process already.

Opposition leaders and critics have argued that those struck off in the exercise include a heavy proportion of minority voters and that Prime Minister Narendra Modi's BJP is using it as a tool to secure future electoral wins. 

The BJP has dismissed such claims, arguing that those removed largely involve dead voters and illegal infiltrators, thereby protecting India's democracy.

A recent report by The Reporter's Collective said about 54.6% of the capital's existing voters had either been struck off the voting list or had been served notices by the ECI, with the burden of proof falling on the voters.

Kerala flash flood: Death toll rises, more rain expected

Search and rescue authorities in the southern state of Kerala found three more dead bodies on Monday morning, taking the death toll from a flash flood to at least five. 

Six people were swept away on Sunday evening in the scenic town of Nilambur when the Kottapuzha River rose due to rainfall higher up in the landscape, local media reported. The search for the sixth person is still on. 

Floods and rain-triggered landslides are a common occurrence in Kerala during the monsoon months, between June and November.

The state's Chief Minister VD Satheesan is set to arrive in the town shortly.

Meanwhile, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) issued orange alerts for six districts in the state on Monday morning, warning of more downpour. 

An orange alert is the second highest in IMD's four-stage color-coded system, sitting just below the red warning. It tells local authorities and residents to 'be prepared' for heavy to very heavy rainfall in the region.

09/21/2026September 21, 2026

Asian Games: India gains four more medal on day 2

Indian athletes picked up two silver and two bronze medals on the second day of the Asian Games 2026 in Japan, taking the nation's tally to six.

Historically, the Asian Games and the Commonwealth Games are where India gains most of its medals at an international level.

Indian shooters Himanshu Dhillon and former world champion Rudrankksh Patil secured India a two-podium finish in the men's 10-meter air rifle event on Monday, winning a silver and a bronze medal respectively.

These medals came barely an hour after they, along with Parth Mane, won a silver in the men's 10-meter air rifle team event for shooting an aggregate qualification score of 1890.1.

30-year-old Suchika Tariyal settled for a bronze in the women's traditional 60 kg mixed martial arts event on Monday after a close-call fight with Singapore's Hui Hoon Teo. Tariyal was seen weeping after the final decision went in Teo's favor on a post-draw technicality where the lighter athlete is awarded the win.

This takes India's overall tally to six medals after picking up two silver medals in the women's 10 meter air rifle competition on Sunday.
