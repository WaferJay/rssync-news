India wraps up Asian Games campaign with 85 medals

India ended its 2026 Asian Games campaign in Aichi-Nagoya with 85 medals – including 21 golds – and finished fourth in the medal table. 

The haul also included 27 silver and 37 bronze medals, putting India ahead of Uzbekistan, which also won 21 golds but fewer silvers. China topped the standings with 169 golds, followed by Japan and South Korea.

The last day of the Games was critical for the country, with India collecting nine medals, five of them gold. 

The hockey teams completed a rare double, with the women beating China 1-0 on Friday and the men defeating Malaysia 5-1 on Saturday. Both sides also secured qualification for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. 

India also won both the men's and women's titles in cricket and kabaddi.

Golfer Pranavi Urs scripted history by becoming the first Indian woman to clinch an Asian Games gold medal in the sport.

Finally, archery and shooting were the biggest medal earners, with archery yielding five golds and shooting 15 medals in total. At 17 years of age, Kumkum Mohod was another breakout star for India, delivering a historic golden double in recurve on her Asian Games debut.

Despite many firsts, the total falls short of India's record haul at Hangzhou in 2023, where the contingent won more than 100 medals, including 28 golds.

Punjab-based queer influencer gunned down in Amritsar

Madhusudan "Mad" Sandhu, a Punjabi makeup artist and LGBTQ social media influencer, was shot dead by two unidentified men on a motorcycle in Amritsar on Saturday evening, local media reported.

Sandhu was attacked around 6 p.m. local time on Loharka Road, outside a car wash center. Bystanders rushed him to a hospital, where he died. 

With over 200,000 followers on Instagram, he was known for speaking openly about makeup artistry, gender issues and LGBTQ rights. He ran a popular beauty salon and beauty academies in Amritsar.

Police registered a case based on a statement from his family. 

"We have leads and will solve the case soon," Prabhjot Singh Virk, the city's Deputy Commissioner of Police (Investigation), said, according to local media. 

While no arrests or motive have been announced, a social media post appeared soon after the shooting, claiming responsibility and accusing the victim of "spoiling" Punjab. This rhetoric was something Sandhu had regularly faced in his public life after being open about his sexuality. 

Authorities said the post claiming responsibility was being looked into as part of the probe, NDTV reported.

Police order probe amid sexual harassment allegations against officers at Delhi protest

Delhi Police have begun an inquiry into complaints by three women journalists who say they were sexually harassed by police personnel while covering protests against India's chief election commissioner. 

In a statement on social media, police said the complaints were referred to the Crime Branch for a "fair and impartial enquiry."

The allegations emerged as police detained protesters near New Delhi's Jantar Mantar for a second day on Saturday, after over 700 people were detained on Friday. Demonstrators have been demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over a mass voter-roll revision that has removed millions of names. The commission denies wrongdoing. 

Kumar has not publicly commented.

In a video shared by Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) founder Abhijeet Dipke, one journalist alleged that an assistant commissioner of police groped her while she filmed the detention of student activist Neha Bora. Police have not publicly responded to the specific allegation. 

Journalists and protesters gathered outside the Parliament Street police station demanding a report be filed, local media reported. 

The Press Club of India expressed "shock and dismay" at the treatment of journalists and urged swift action. Opposition leader Rahul Gandhi asked Home Minister Amit Shah to respond, noting that Delhi Police fall under his ministry.

Several student groups have called for a march from India Gate to Jantar Mantar on Sunday at 3 p.m. local time. 

Meanwhile, opposition parties plan to march on the Election Commission on Tuesday, and the CJP has called for a mass mobilization in Delhi on October 10.

Indian men join women's hockey team in securing 2028 Olympics berth

India's men's hockey team beat Malaysia 5-1 in Saturday's final to retain the Asian Games title, marking the first time the team has won back-to-back golds at the Games.

This came a day after India's women's team delivered one of the standout results of the Games, edging defending champions China 1-0 in Friday's final to end a 44-year wait for gold. 

Forward Lalremsiami Hmarzote's goal in the 10th minute, backed by goalkeeper Savita Punia saving a penalty stroke from China's Zhang Ying before halftime, helped secure the win. 

The victory was India's first women's hockey gold at the Asian Games since 1982 and also secured the team a direct qualification spot for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.

Pakistan summons Indian envoy over border killing of 2 civilians

Pakistan summoned India's charge d'affaires in Islamabad on Saturday to lodge a "strong protest" over the alleged killing of two Pakistani civilians by India's Border Security Force (BSF) near the border in the Kasur district of Pakistan's Punjab.

Pakistan's Foreign Office said two unarmed civilians were killed and a third injured when BSF personnel opened fire in the Bedian sector on Friday, calling it "unprovoked and lethal force."

While there was no immediate comment from New Delhi, Indian media reported a conflicting account, saying the men were suspected of trying to enter the country.

Pakistan rejected any characterization of the men as "infiltrators."

The incident comes against a tense backdrop of a historically fraught relationship between the neighbors. It occurred a day after Pakistan's military publicly disputed an earlier Indian claim regarding a separate incident in Indian-administered Kashmir.

India beat Pakistan by 19 runs to retain Asian Games cricket gold

India successfully defended their Asian Games men's cricket gold medal, beating rivals Pakistan by 19 runs in Saturday's final in Nisshin, Japan.

After posting 211-6 backed by Abhishek Sharma's 61 off 20 balls and a late effort from Tilak Varma and Shivam Dube, India restricted Pakistan to 192-6 despite a big knock of 96 off 51 balls from Hasan Nawaz. 

Nawaz fell four runs short of a century on the final ball of the innings, when he was caught by wicketkeeper Sanju Samson off Dube's bowling. It marked Pakistan's first-ever appearance in a men's Asian Games cricket final.

The event also marked one of the game's fiercest rivalries played out on an unlikely stage: a converted baseball field in suburban Nagoya, where Indians and Pakistanis living in Japan queued for hours to fill the 2,500-capacity venue. 

"I think this is never going to happen again, India against Pakistan in Japan," Pakistan supporter Umer Dar, a 26-year-old Nagoya resident, told AFP.

"Even if it's in Japan, the craze of India versus Pakistan is always there," added Tokyo-based India fan Afroz Khan. "Anywhere they play in the world, it's always crazy."

While cricket remains a niche sport in Japan, there has been growing local interest, as shown by India's first-ever T20 international against Japan last week, which India won by just two runs in a rain-shortened match.

Pranavi Urs scripts history, wins Asian Games golf gold

Seerat Chabba

Pranavi Urs has become the first Indian woman to win gold in the Asian Games individual golf after she sank a nerve-wracking three-foot par putt on the final green to clinch the title by one stroke, finishing at 15-under par. 

Teammates Aditi Ashok and Diksha Dagar celebrated by soaking Urs with water as her win was confirmed.

China's Yin Ruoning and Singapore's Shannon Tan tied for silver and bronze a stroke back, with Yin claiming silver after a playoff.

This is India's first gold in the sport since Shiv Kapur's men's title in 2002. Urs, Ashok and Dagar also came together to win India its first-ever team silver in the event. China won team gold, with Taiwan's team, featuring five-time major champion Tseng Ya-ni, taking bronze.

10/03/2026October 3, 2026

CJP calls for mass Delhi march on October 10

Seerat Chabba

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) has announced it will mobilize a nationwide march on New Delhi next weekend unless Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar resigns.

The call follows a protest at Mumbai's Shivaji Park that drew thousands of demonstrators despite police denying permission for the gathering.

A similar protest at New Delhi's Jantar Mantar saw several opposition politicians and student leaders being detained on Friday, which is also observed as Gandhi Jayanti to honor the birth of Mahatma Gandhi, a global pioneer of nonviolence.

"If Gyanesh Kumar doesn't resign, cockroaches from across India will march to Delhi and protest at Jantar Mantar on October 10," CJP founder and convener Abhijeet Dipke said in the statement. 

"And if the Delhi Police tries to stop us, wherever they stop us will become our Jantar Mantar. Ours will be a peaceful protest. We will walk on the path shown by Mahatma Gandhi." 

Calling on people to join the march to preserve free and fair elections in the country, Dipke called it "a fight to save our democracy."

The announcement follows a tense October 2, when Delhi Police detained more than 700 demonstrators, including former Delhi Chief Minister Atishi, from Jantar Mantar and surrounding areas, local media reported. 

Police had denied permission for the Delhi gathering and imposed Section 163 restrictions barring assemblies of more than five people.

The CJP-led gathering in Mumbai's Shivaji Park also saw a huge turnout, including film actors and musicians, but did not face a similar violent crackdown from authorities. 

It is the latest flashpoint in a months-long movement that began as online satire in May and escalated into repeated street demonstrations, including a July 20 protest where Delhi Police used batons to disperse crowds. It led to the resignation of India's then-Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan in late July.
